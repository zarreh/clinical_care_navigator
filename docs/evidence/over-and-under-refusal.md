# Over- and under-refusal

*Arrives in Phase 8.*

This is the headline chart, and it has two lines on it because either one alone
is meaningless.

**Under-refusal** is the dangerous direction: the system answered something it
should have declined or escalated. Every individual miss is listed with its
text, never summarised into a rate and left there.

**Over-refusal** is the direction nobody measures: the system declined, escalated
or queued something perfectly ordinary. It is not dangerous, and it is the reason
safety systems get switched off. A guardrail that refuses *"what does my A1c
mean?"* has a spotless safety record and no users.

You can drive either number to zero by ruining the other. Reporting them
together, at each autonomy level, is the only honest presentation — and the
shape of that trade-off at L1 / L2 / L3 is what a clinical owner is actually
choosing between.
